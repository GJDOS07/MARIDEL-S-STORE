About Maridel's Store

Familiar favourites, thoughtfully stocked for your home.

Established in 2021, Maridel's Store brings together the authentic flavors, beloved pantry staples, and everyday household essentials for families across Australia.

From jasmine rice to familiar noodles and condiments, we pack every parcel with utmost care so you can cook the dishes that make home feel close.

100% Authentic Genuine Asian brands and certified staples.
Australia-Wide Reliable express dispatch to Sydney, Melbourne, Perth & more.
Our Commitment

Good products.
Warm service.

Whether you are cooking a celebratory family meal or looking for comfort snacks after work, Maridel's Store is your trusted pantry partner.
