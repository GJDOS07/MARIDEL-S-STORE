About Maridel's Store

Familiar favourites, thoughtfully stocked for your home.

Established in 2021, Maridel's Store brings together the authentic flavors, beloved pantry staples, and everyday household essentials for the Filipino community across Australia.

From jasmine rice to familiar merienda noodles and condiments, we pack every parcel with utmost care so you can cook the dishes that make home feel close.

100% Authentic Genuine Philippine brands and certified staples.
